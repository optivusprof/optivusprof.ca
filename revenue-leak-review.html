What you get

  • A 6-page leak memo. Your three biggest revenue leaks, ranked, with an estimate of what each one costs you a year and the evidence behind it.
  • A 30-day fix plan. The first three moves for each leak, an owner for each move, and the number that will tell you it worked.
  • Your 10-point leak scorecard. Speed to response, lead qualification, pipeline coverage, win rate, sales cycle, CAC payback, content distinctiveness, AI search visibility, data readiness and retention, scored on your real numbers.
  • The voice of your market. Short calls with three of your customers and two prospects who chose someone else, so the findings are not just my opinion or yours.
  • A 60-minute readout. With you, your sales lead and, if you like, your board member or PE partner.

How the 10 days run

  1. Before day 1: the free audit and three data pullsYou complete the 6-minute Revenue Engine Audit and send a CRM pipeline export, 12 months of marketing spend by channel, and your last 10 won and 10 lost deals.
  2. Days 1 to 2: working session90 minutes with you and your sales lead. How deals really get won, where they stall, and what the team believes is broken.
  3. Days 3 to 7: analysis and market callsI work through the data and run five short calls with customers and lost prospects.
  4. Days 8 to 9: the memoThree leaks, a dollar estimate for each, and the 30-day plan.
  5. Day 10: readout60 minutes. You leave knowing what to fix first, who owns it and how you will know it worked.

Who it is for

A strong fit

  • B2B companies between $15M and $60M in revenue
  • A sales team, and at least a year of data in a CRM
  • Growth has stalled, or a new target has arrived with no plan behind it
  • You are about to hire a marketing leader or change agencies

Not a fit

  • Under $10M in revenue with no sales team yet
  • You want someone to run campaigns, ads or social
  • No one senior can give it four hours over two weeks

What happens after the Review

You can fix the leaks with your own team using the 30-day plan. If you want help running the fix, the next step is a 90-day Strategy Sprint at $8,000 a month: your ideal customer, positioning and channel plan in writing within 30 to 45 days, then 60 days running it with your team. After that, a Revenue Architect retainer of $6,000 to $12,000 a month with a 6-month minimum, if it is working. Your $3,500 comes off the first Sprint invoice.
