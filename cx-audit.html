The Problem

You are losing revenue at stages you cannot see

Most B2B companies focus marketing investment on top-of-funnel awareness. The revenue leakage - the deals that stall, the prospects that go cold, the customers that churn quietly - happens deeper in the funnel and gets far less attention. Every friction point you haven't measured is a quiet tax on your growth. A CX audit maps the full journey and identifies exactly where the revenue is going.

Who This Is For

Companies with a funnel that works — but not well enough

B2B companies that have an established sales and marketing motion but are seeing unexplained drop-off, longer-than-expected sales cycles, poor retention, or expansion revenue that never materializes. You are generating demand. Something is converting it poorly.

"The most expensive marketing problem most B2B companies have is not getting leads. It is losing the leads they already have to friction they have never measured."

AI-Assisted Delivery

CRM notes, chat logs, email threads, and call transcripts hold most of the evidence of where a journey breaks down - far more than any consultant can read manually. AI-assisted analysis surfaces friction across all of it, so the audit finds patterns a manual review would miss and sizes each one with a dollar estimate.

Frequently Asked Questions

CRM pipeline data, marketing automation reports, website analytics, and ideally 3–5 customer interviews. The audit is more powerful with data but can generate meaningful findings from qualitative research alone.

Four weeks from kick-off to final presentation. The first two weeks focus on data gathering and mapping. The second two weeks focus on analysis and recommendations.

A standard marketing audit looks at marketing activities and channels. The CX audit looks at the buyer experience end-to-end — including what happens after marketing hands off to sales, and what happens after the deal closes. It finds the revenue that a channel-focused audit misses.

The deliverable is a prioritized action list, not a general report. The top 10 fixes are sequenced by revenue impact and implementation effort. Most clients implement the first three within 30 days and see measurable pipeline improvement within 60.
