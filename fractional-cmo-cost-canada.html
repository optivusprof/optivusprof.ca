The direct answer: how much does a fractional CMO cost in Canada?

Direct answer - 2025:

A fractional CMO in Canada costs $6,000–$12,000 per month on a retainer basis. The range reflects scope: days per week, whether the CMO manages a team, and the complexity of the marketing function. Fixed-fee project engagements (like a 30-day GTM sprint) run $15,000–$30,000. This compares to $250,000–$350,000 in total annual compensation for a full-time CMO - making fractional 20–30% of the full-time cost.

What drives fractional CMO pricing in Canada?

Five factors determine where a fractional CMO retainer lands within the $6,000–$12,000 range:

  1. Days per week. One day per week (light strategic advisory) sits at the lower end. Two to three days per week (full CMO function including team management) sits at the higher end. Most Optivus engagements run 2–2.5 days per week.
  2. Scope of responsibility. Strategy-only engagements (no team management, no agency oversight) cost less. Full CMO function - managing in-house team, briefing agencies, owning pipeline targets, attending all leadership meetings - costs more.
  3. Company size and complexity. A $7M company with one marketing coordinator is simpler than a $60M company with a 6-person team and 3 agency relationships. Complexity drives scope drives cost.
  4. Experience and track record. A fractional CMO with 5 years of experience costs less than one with 25+ years of VP-level results at TELUS, TEC Canada, Veriforce, and SMART Technologies. You're pricing the pattern recognition and the proven results, not just the hours.
  5. Geographic market. Toronto and Vancouver fractional CMOs typically command 10–20% premium over Calgary rates due to higher local salary benchmarks. Optivus Calgary pricing reflects the Alberta market while delivering national and international experience.

Is a fractional CMO cheaper than a marketing agency?

For strategic work - yes, usually, and significantly more effective. A full-service B2B marketing agency typically costs $8,000–$25,000/month and delivers execution outputs: content, campaigns, ads, and reports. They don't own your strategy, they don't manage your internal team, and they don't attend your leadership meetings.

A fractional CMO at $6,000–$12,000/month delivers the strategy that makes execution effective - and can manage the agency relationship on your behalf, ensuring every dollar spent on execution is pulling in the right direction. Many high-performing B2B companies run both: a fractional CMO for strategy and leadership, and an agency for execution capacity.
